Formula Quick Reference

Essential formulas for the General exam

Ohm's Law

E = I × R I = E / R R = E / I
E = Voltage (Volts) | I = Current (Amps) | R = Resistance (Ohms)

Power Law

P = E × I P = I² × R P = E² / R
P = Power (Watts)

Wavelength

λ (meters) = 300 / f (MHz)

Antenna Length

Half-wave dipole (feet) = 468 / f (MHz) Quarter-wave vertical (feet) = 234 / f (MHz)

Decibels

+3 dB = double power -3 dB = half power +10 dB = 10× power -10 dB = 1/10 power dB = 10 × log₁₀(P₂/P₁)

Reactance

X_L = 2πfL (inductive) X_C = 1/(2πfC) (capacitive)
f = frequency | L = inductance (H) | C = capacitance (F)

Impedance

Z = √(R² + X²) SWR = Z_load / Z_line (or inverse)

Resonant Frequency

f = 1 / (2π√(LC))

Transformer Ratio

V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p Z ratio = (N_s/N_p)²
V = voltage | N = turns | Z = impedance
